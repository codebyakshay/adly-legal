About Adly

Adly is a mobile advertising management application developed by Codebyakshay. Adly empowers digital marketers, business owners, and advertisers to connect their authorized advertising assets and seamlessly view, monitor, and manage campaigns through third-party platform interfaces, specifically utilizing APIs provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (including Meta/Facebook Login and the Meta Marketing API).

We are committed to full transparency, data protection, and user control. Review our legal documentation below to understand how your information is handled and the terms governing your use of Adly.

Our Key Principles

  • Data Minimization: We handle only information strictly necessary to authenticate your account and display authorized campaign assets. Adly does not collect sensitive personal data such as precise location, personal contacts, health, or financial information.
  • User-Granted Permissions Only: Adly accesses only Meta advertising accounts and assets that you explicitly authorize via Meta's standard OAuth dialogs.
  • No Data Sales: Adly never sells, rents, or monetizes your personal data.
  • Data Deletion Support: Users can request complete deletion of their Adly data by emailing codebyakshay@codebyakshay.com. We also support automated Meta data-deletion callback requests.
  • Platform Independence: Adly is an independent application developed by Codebyakshay and is not owned, operated, sponsored, or endorsed by Meta Platforms, Inc.
